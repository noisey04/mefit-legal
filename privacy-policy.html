MeFit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 Developer: Mayank Enterprises (Priyesh Mayank) Contact: mayankent0@gmail.com App: MeFit – Gym Workout Log (package com.priyeshmayank.tallyset)

Summary

MeFit needs a Google sign-in. It uses your account to back up your workout data privately, so you can restore it on a new phone. MeFit does not sell or share your data, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

What we collect

Account information (from Google sign-in) - Your name, email address and a Google/Firebase account ID. - Used only to create and identify your MeFit account and show you which account you are signed in with.

Your fitness data - Routines, exercises (including custom exercises) and workout logs: weights, reps, RPE/RIR, rest times, notes, dates and durations. - Optional body metrics you choose to enter: body weight, waist, body fat %, sleep hours, steps and notes. - If you have a coach (2.12+): your diet log against the coach's chart (meals eaten or swapped, extra foods and their protein). - Weekly check-in notes and app settings (units, theme, goal, target weight, timer preferences).

This data is stored on your phone and a copy is backed up to your account. It is used only to show your data back to you, and to restore and sync it across your devices.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • Progress photos and your profile picture (optional) are stored only on your phone. They are never backed up or exported, and are never shared except progress photos you choose to share with your own coach (see Coach mode below), and are removed if you uninstall the app, use Erase all data, or delete your account.
  • In your cloud backup, in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google as our service provider. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Security rules let only your signed-in account read or write your backup; the developer does not use it for anything else.

When data leaves your phone

Train together (optional, 2.5+). If you pair with a buddy using a one-time code, that buddy can see your first name and, while you train, your current workout: exercise names, sets, reps, weights (you can hide weights), personal bests, and whether you have finished. They can send you a fixed reaction (Fire or Flex). Up to 10 buddies. Nothing is shared unless you pair; you can hide any workout or unlink at any time, and deleting your account removes your live card and links. From version 2.11, Share my plan with buddies is on by default for people you pair with: they can see your active routine (days, exercises, sets, rep ranges and rest; never weights, history or notes) and copy it. You can turn it off any time in Profile → Buddies, and nothing is shared with anyone you have not paired with. Your location, photos and body weight are never shared with buddies. Buddy invites can be sent as a link (noisey04.github.io/join); the link only carries the one-time code.

Coach mode (optional, 2.12+). A trainer can turn on Coach mode and invite clients with a one-time link or code (valid 7 days).

  • If you join a coach, that coach can see your name and your completed workouts from the last 8 weeks (exercises, sets, reps, weights, effort and workout notes). You choose whether they can also see your diet log against their chart, your body weight, and your progress photos; progress photos are off unless you turn them on. You can change these choices, delete shared photos, or leave the coach at any time in Profile → My coach. Leaving removes the coach's access and deletes the shared copies; your own data stays on your phone and in your backup.
  • Shared progress photos are uploaded to Cloud Firestore only while photo sharing is on, readable only by you and your coach, and deleted when you delete them, switch sharing off and delete them, leave the coach, or delete your account. They are never shown to buddies.
  • If you are a coach, your clients see the name and gym or city you enter, the routines (with any starting weights) and diet charts you send them, and your comments.
  • Security rules allow only the coach and the client in each link to read that link's data. A client can have one coach at a time.
  • Cloud backup: automatically, while you are signed in, as described above.
  • Export / share: If you tap Export backup (JSON), Export sets (CSV), or Copy / Share on the weekly summary, the app hands a file or text to Android so you can choose where it goes. What happens next is governed by the app you choose.

What MeFit does NOT do

  • No sale of data, and no sharing with third parties for their own use
  • No advertising or advertising ID
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No location, contacts, microphone or Health Connect access
  • No camera or photo-library access unless you choose to add a photo; Android's own picker or camera app hands MeFit only the one photo you pick, and it stays on your phone unless you share it with your coach

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — for Google sign-in and the cloud backup.
  • Vibrate — to buzz when a rest timer ends (you can turn this off in Settings).
  • Notifications (optional, asked only when you turn on reminders) and run at startup — for training-day reminders, which are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data and account

  • Profile → Delete account in the app permanently deletes your MeFit account, your cloud backup, your buddy and coach links (including anything shared through them) and the data on that phone.
  • Profile → Erase all data clears all your fitness data on the phone and in your backup, but keeps your account.
  • Without the app: follow the steps at https://noisey04.github.io/mefit-legal/delete-account.html, or email mayankent0@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We delete the account and backup within 30 days.
  • Deleted data is not kept; Firebase may hold it in encrypted backups for a short period before it is removed.

Children

MeFit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Health information

Body metrics and training logs may be considered health or fitness information. They are used only to show them back to you and to back them up. MeFit does not provide medical advice.
