MeFit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 Developer: Mayank Enterprises (Priyesh Mayank) Contact: mayankent0@gmail.com App: MeFit – Gym Workout Log (package com.priyeshmayank.tallyset)

Summary

MeFit needs a Google sign-in. It uses your account to back up your workout data privately, so you can restore it on a new phone. MeFit does not sell or share your data, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

What we collect

Account information (from Google sign-in) - Your name, email address and a Google/Firebase account ID. - Used only to create and identify your MeFit account and show you which account you are signed in with.

Your fitness data - Routines, exercises (including custom exercises) and workout logs: weights, reps, RPE/RIR, rest times, notes, dates and durations. - Optional body metrics you choose to enter: body weight, waist, body fat %, sleep hours, steps and notes. - If you have a coach (2.12+): your diet log against the coach's chart (meals eaten or swapped, extra foods and their protein). - Weekly check-in notes and app settings (units, theme, goal, target weight, timer preferences).

This data is stored on your phone and a copy is backed up to your account. It is used only to show your data back to you, and to restore and sync it across your devices.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • Progress photos and your profile picture (optional) are stored only on your phone. They are never backed up or exported, and are never shared except progress photos you choose to share with your own coach (see Coach mode below), and are removed if you uninstall the app, use Erase all data, or delete your account.
  • In your cloud backup, in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google as our service provider. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Security rules let only your signed-in account read or write your backup; the developer does not use it for anything else.

When data leaves your phone

Train together (optional, 2.5+). If you pair with a buddy using a one-time code, that buddy can see your first name and, while you train, your current workout: exercise names, sets, reps, weights (you can hide weights), personal bests, and whether you have finished. They can send you a fixed reaction (Fire or Flex). Up to 30 buddies (10 before version 2.25). Nothing is shared unless you pair; you can hide any workout or unlink at any time, and deleting your account removes your live card and links. From version 2.11, Share my plan with buddies is on by default for people you pair with: they can see your active routine (days, exercises, sets, rep ranges and rest; never weights, history or notes) and copy it. You can turn it off any time in Profile → Buddies, and nothing is shared with anyone you have not paired with. Your location, photos and body weight are never shared with buddies. Buddy invites can be sent as a link (noisey04.github.io/join); the link only carries the one-time code.

Coach mode (optional, 2.12+). A trainer can turn on Coach mode and invite clients with a one-time link or code (valid 7 days).

  • If you join a coach, that coach can see your name and your completed workouts from the last 8 weeks (exercises, sets, reps, weights, effort and workout notes). You choose whether they can also see your diet log against their chart, your body weight, and your progress photos; progress photos are off unless you turn them on. You can change these choices, delete shared photos, or leave the coach at any time in Profile → My coach. Leaving removes the coach's access and deletes the shared copies; your own data stays on your phone and in your backup.
  • Shared progress photos are uploaded to Cloud Firestore only while photo sharing is on, readable only by you and your coach, and deleted when you delete them, switch sharing off and delete them, leave the coach, or delete your account. They are never shown to buddies.
  • If you are a coach, your clients see the name and gym or city you enter, the routines (with any starting weights) and diet charts you send them, and your comments.
  • Security rules allow only the coach and the client in each link to read that link's data. A client can have one coach at a time.

Groups and challenges (optional, 2.16+). You can create or join a group of up to 20 people with an invite link or code (valid 14 days), and start or join challenges with buddies or a group. From 2.25, new groups can no longer be created or joined: Progress → Buddies shows one leaderboard of the buddies you have paired with. Groups you were already in keep working until you leave them, and nothing more is shared with them than before.

  • In a group, the other members see your first name, how many workouts you did this week, the date you last trained and whether you are training right now. The group's name and weekly goal are visible to its members.
  • In a challenge, the people in it see your first name and your score for that challenge only: number of workouts, number of active days, number of sets, or your best percentage improvement in estimated maximum on a lift. Your phone works out your score; the workouts themselves are not shared.
  • Nothing else is shared: not your weights, exercises, notes, photos, body weight or location. Security rules allow only the members of a group or challenge to read it.
  • You can leave any group or challenge at any time. The person who created a group can remove members or delete the group; the person who started a challenge can delete it. Deleting your account removes you from all groups and challenges and deletes the ones you created.
  • From 2.17, if you have connected steps and Share daily steps is on, group members also see today's step total, and a Most steps challenge shows your step total for the challenge dates.
  • From 2.21, if you choose Share my lifts for a group (it is off until you choose), that group's members see your estimated maximum on up to 6 of your most-trained lifts, from now and about 6 weeks ago, and the type of equipment. You choose this for each group separately and can switch it off on the group's page; switching off removes it.

Share my progress with buddies (optional, 2.16.1+). If you switch this on in Profile → Buddies, your phone sends your paired buddies a short summary so they can compare progress with you: workouts per week for the last 12 weeks, the number of workouts, sets and personal bests in the last 30 days and, if you also switch on Lifts, your estimated maximum per week on each lift (from 2.25, up to 12 lifts, grouped by movement so that, for example, any bench press counts as Bench press). Your buddies see these on a leaderboard with you and their other buddies who share the same. Your buddies' summaries are shown to you the same way. Workouts themselves, notes, photos, body weight and location are not included. Switch it off at any time and the summary is removed; unlinking a buddy or deleting your account removes it too.

Notifications (2.22+). If you turn on buddy alerts, MeFit checks about every 15 minutes in the background for updates your buddies already share with you (that they started a workout, their progress summary) and shows a notification on your phone. The missed-workout reminder is worked out on your phone from your own workouts. Nothing extra is collected or shared, and you can turn each alert off in Profile → Reminders and alerts.

Steps from Health Connect (optional, 2.17+)

  • If you tap Connect Health Connect in Profile → Steps and activity and allow it, MeFit reads only your daily step total (permission READ_STEPS) from Health Connect, where apps such as Samsung Health, Google Fit or Fitbit save it, for the last 30 days and while MeFit is open. MeFit does not read heart rate, sleep, location or any other Health Connect data, and never writes to Health Connect.
  • Step totals are stored on your phone only. They are not in your cloud backup.
  • If Share daily steps is on (you can switch it off any time), your daily totals for the last 30 days are added to the progress summary your paired buddies see, and today's total is shown to groups you are in. Nothing is shared with anyone you have not paired with or joined.
  • Step data is used only to show you and the people you choose your steps. It is never used for advertising, sold, or used to make decisions about you.
  • To stop: tap Stop reading steps (this clears steps from your phone and from what you share), or remove MeFit's access in Health Connect → App permissions.
  • MeFit's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Runs, walks, rides and swims (optional, 2.27+)

  • When you track a run or walk, MeFit counts distance from your phone's own step counter (permission Physical activity, asked on your first tracked run). MeFit does not use GPS or your location.
  • While a run or walk is being tracked, an ongoing notification shows time, distance and steps, so tracking keeps going with the screen off. It stops when you end or discard the session.
  • The time, distance, steps, splits and effort of each session are saved with your workouts and backed up like any other workout. They are not shared with anyone unless you share them yourself.
  • Race plans (a hybrid fitness race or triathlon) and race-simulation splits are saved in your settings and workouts in the same way.

Heart rate from a Bluetooth monitor (optional, 2.28+)

  • If you connect a heart rate monitor in Profile → Heart rate monitor (for example WHOOP with Broadcast Heart Rate on, or a chest strap), MeFit reads your live heart rate over Bluetooth while a workout, run or race simulation is open.
  • MeFit uses Android's Nearby devices permission for this, set so it is never used to work out your location.
  • For each workout MeFit saves a summary: average and maximum heart rate, time in each heart rate zone, and an estimated calorie count (worked out from your heart rate, the age you enter and your latest body weight). The summary is saved with the workout and backed up. Second-by-second heart rate stays in the phone's memory and is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • MeFit does not connect to your WHOOP account or any other company's servers; it only listens to the heart rate your monitor broadcasts.
  • To stop: tap Forget in Profile → Heart rate monitor, or remove the Nearby devices permission in Android settings. Heart rate summaries are deleted with the workout or with Erase all data.
  • Cloud backup: automatically, while you are signed in, as described above.
  • Export / share: If you tap Export backup (JSON), Export sets (CSV), or Copy / Share on the weekly summary, the app hands a file or text to Android so you can choose where it goes. What happens next is governed by the app you choose.

What MeFit does NOT do

  • No sale of data, and no sharing with third parties for their own use
  • No advertising or advertising ID
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No location (no GPS), contacts or microphone access, and no Health Connect data other than daily steps when you connect them (see above)
  • No camera or photo-library access unless you choose to add a photo; Android's own picker or camera app hands MeFit only the one photo you pick, and it stays on your phone unless you share it with your coach

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — for Google sign-in and the cloud backup.
  • Vibrate — to buzz when a rest timer ends (you can turn this off in Settings).
  • Read steps (Health Connect) (optional, asked only when you connect steps) — to show your daily steps.
  • Physical activity (optional, asked on your first tracked run or walk) — to count distance from your phone's step counter. Foreground service and keep awake — to keep counting with the screen off while you track, with an ongoing notification.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth) (optional, asked when you search for a heart rate monitor) — to read live heart rate from a monitor you choose. Never used for location.
  • Notifications (optional, asked only when you turn on reminders) and run at startup — for training-day reminders, which are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data and account

  • Profile → Delete account in the app permanently deletes your MeFit account, your cloud backup, your buddy and coach links (including anything shared through them), your group and challenge memberships (and the groups and challenges you created) and the data on that phone.
  • Profile → Erase all data clears all your fitness data on the phone and in your backup, but keeps your account.
  • Without the app: follow the steps at https://noisey04.github.io/mefit-legal/delete-account.html, or email mayankent0@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We delete the account and backup within 30 days.
  • Deleted data is not kept; Firebase may hold it in encrypted backups for a short period before it is removed.

Children

MeFit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Health information

Body metrics, training logs, step counts and heart rate summaries may be considered health or fitness information. They are used only to show them back to you and to back them up. MeFit does not provide medical advice.
