Delete your MeFit account

App: MeFit – Gym Workout Log, by Mayank Enterprises

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open MeFit and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to Your data and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Google may ask you to pick your account again.

This permanently deletes your MeFit account, your cloud backup and the data on that phone, straight away.

Without the app

Email mayankent0@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my MeFit account". We delete your account and cloud backup within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Delete your data but keep your account

In MeFit, go to Profile → Your data → Erase all data and confirm. This deletes all your routines, workouts, sets, body metrics and notes, both on the phone and in your cloud backup. Your account (name, email, account ID) stays, so you can start fresh. You can also email mayankent0@gmail.com to ask for specific data to be deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and account ID
  • Your cloud backup: routines, workouts, sets, body metrics, notes and settings

Nothing is kept afterwards. Firebase may hold deleted data in encrypted backups for a short period before it is removed.

Want to keep your data first? In the app, tap Profile → Export backup (JSON) before deleting.
